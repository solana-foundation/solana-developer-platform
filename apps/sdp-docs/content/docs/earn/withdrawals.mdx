title: Withdraw funds
description: Prepare a withdrawal from a position, choose its exit route when offered, and track the payout.
---

Send `POST /v1/earn/withdraw` with a position from
[`GET /v1/earn/positions`](/docs/earn/balances):

```json title="withdrawal.json"
{
  "positionId": "<position-id>",
  "amount": { "kind": "all" }
}
```

The position fixes the strategy and the owner, and the payout goes back to that
owner. To check its balance first, [read the position](/docs/earn/balances#read-one-position).
`slippageBps` is optional and sits at the top level. See
[all withdrawal parameters](/docs/reference/api/earn/prepare-earn-withdrawal).

| `amount.kind` | Withdraws |
| --- | --- |
| `all` | The whole position. |
| `shares` | `value` shares. |
| `token` | Shares worth `value` of the strategy's deposit token `mint`. |
| `intermediate` | `value` of a held intermediate token `mint`. Some strategies redeem in two steps, through a token you hold in between. |

## Choose an exit route

An instant exit burns the shares and pays out in one transaction. A queued
exit files a withdrawal request instead: the shares move into the provider's
escrow until the provider pays out, which can take days.

When a strategy offers both, `nextAction.type` is `select_option`, and
`terms.options` lists each route with its `settlement`, `estimatedSeconds` and
`amount`. Call
`POST /v1/operations/{operationId}/refresh` with the chosen `optionId` and the
current `actionId` and `version`. SDP prepares that route's transaction for you
to review, and the route stays fixed on later refreshes.

While the withdrawal waits for your choice, `canCancel` is `true` and
`POST /v1/operations/{operationId}/cancel` abandons it. Cancel needs the Earn
**Manage** permission (`earn.manage`).

## Sign, submit and track

Sign and submit as for a [deposit](/docs/earn/deposits#sign-and-submit), then
poll the operation:

- A queued withdrawal shows `submitted` until its request confirms, then
  `settling` until the provider pays out. Poll it every few minutes, not
  seconds: `redemptionDelayDays` on the strategy estimates the wait.
- A request ID or escrowed shares aren't a payout, and there is no separate
  claim step.
- `completed` means SDP verified the payout. A `null` `result.settledAmount` is
  unknown, not zero.

Once submitted, a withdrawal can't be refreshed or cancelled, even while
queued. If it ends `failed`, prepare a new one with a new `Idempotency-Key`; an
unknown outcome is not a failure. If shares get stuck in an expired queue
request, [recover them](/docs/earn/integration-details#recover-expired-withdrawal-escrow).
