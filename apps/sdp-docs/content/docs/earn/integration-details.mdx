## Idempotency and recovery

Persist a fresh `Idempotency-Key` and request body before every mutation. Reuse
both after an uncertain response. Find accepted work with `GET /v1/operations`
and recover its next action instead of creating another deposit or withdrawal.

## Reviewed actions

Submit names the exact `actionId` and `version`. Owner-signed transactions
must match the prepared message and all required signatures. A later step can
require another review under the same operation.

## Refresh safely

Call `POST /v1/operations/{operationId}/refresh` with the current `actionId` and
`version` only when the operation permits preparation. For `select_option`,
include an advertised `optionId`; review the new transaction before `/submit`.
Later refreshes preserve the selected withdrawal route.

Timeout, blockhash expiry and missing RPC history do not prove
non-execution. Exposed owner-signed messages retain independent-broadcast
uncertainty even before SDP receives a signature.

## Source and approval

A source uses a registered wallet ID or owner address. A provider can require
approval outside your app; render the operation's instructions and supplied
handoff URL. Approval policy belongs to the wallet, independent of whether
Treasury or Embedded Yield initiated the operation.

## Provider eligibility

Permissioned assets can require separate KYC, KYB and wallet registration.
The internal provider eligibility routes establish those requirements; they do
not replace the core deposit, withdrawal or operation endpoints.
