module: earn
title: Deposit funds
description: Prepare and review strategy deposits, then follow approval and settlement.
---

Send `POST /v1/earn/deposit` with `strategyId`, a source and token amount.
The source contains exactly one `walletId` or `ownerAddress`; amounts use a mint
and decimal-string `value` in that token's units.

## Review before submission

Preparation returns `202` with a durable operation, economic terms and its next
action. It performs quotes, planning and simulation without signing, opening
approval or broadcasting. Show the estimate, enforceable minimum and fees.
Unknown fees are not zero fees.

For `sign_transaction`, have every required signer sign the exact advertised
message. For `submit`, follow the registered wallet's configured policy.
Call `POST /v1/operations/{operationId}/submit` with the current action ID and
version, including signed bytes when required. Submission authorizes and queues
that exact transaction; it does not prepare another action.

Funding with another supported token combines the swap and deposit into one
transaction. If they cannot fit together after a compact-route retry, preparation
refuses before signing. Fund the strategy's deposit token and retry; SDP never
returns a standalone funding swap.

The worker handles signing, provider approval and submission. Poll
`GET /v1/operations/{operationId}` and display its next action. A queued or
submitted operation has not yet established delivery.

Persist an `Idempotency-Key` before each mutation. Recover uncertain responses
by reading the operation and retrying the same body with its original key.
