The [quickstart](/docs/earn/quickstart) walks through one deposit end to end.
This page covers the details.

## Prepare

`POST /v1/earn/deposit` takes the strategy, the owner's wallet and the amount.
See [all deposit parameters](/docs/reference/api/earn/prepare-earn-deposit).

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `strategyId` | From [discovery](/docs/earn/strategies). |
| `source.ownerAddress` | The wallet that signs and owns the position. It needs no registration in SDP. |
| `amount` | The `mint` you fund with, and `value` as a decimal string in whole tokens: `"10"` is 10 USDC, not base units. |
| `slippageBps` | Optional, at the top level. `50` is 0.5%, at most `500`. Defaults to the strategy's `depositSlippage.defaultToleranceBps`, else 50. |
| `feePayer` | Optional address that pays network fees instead of the owner. It must also sign. |

Funding with a token other than the strategy's deposit token puts the swap and
the deposit in one transaction, so a deposit never half-executes. If the two
don't fit in one transaction, preparation fails before anything is signed:
fund the deposit token directly and try again.

## Review

Preparation returns `202` with an [operation](/docs/earn/api-overview#operation).
SDP quotes, plans and simulates the transaction, but signs and sends nothing.
Show your customer:

- `terms.estimate`: the quoted shares.
- `terms.minimum`: the least the transaction accepts.
- `terms.fees`: the known fees. `null` means unknown, not free.

## Sign and submit

The owner, and the `feePayer` if you set one, sign `nextAction.transaction`, a
v0 transaction, without changing it. Sign only, don't send it: SDP broadcasts
what you submit. Send the signed transaction, base64-encoded, with the
operation's `actionId` and `version` to `POST /v1/operations/{operationId}/submit`.
SDP checks the bytes match what it prepared, then broadcasts them.

If the customer declines to sign, nothing executes and no SDP call is needed.
The operation stays `prepared`, and `status=active` keeps listing it. Once the
network passes its `lastValidBlockHeight`, its transaction can't land, so you
can prepare a new deposit.

## Track

Poll `GET /v1/operations/{operationId}` until `status` is `completed`, `failed`
or `cancelled`; anything else is still in progress. A submitted deposit can't
be refreshed or cancelled, so keep tracking it. After you submit, only a
`failed` deposit lets you prepare a new one, with a new `Idempotency-Key`. A
timeout or an unknown outcome does not:
[retry the same request](/docs/earn/integration-details#retries-and-idempotency)
instead.
