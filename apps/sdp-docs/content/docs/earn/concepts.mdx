## Strategy

A strategy is an entry in the Earn catalogue. Its provider may implement a
vault, lending market or RWA product. A protocol can expose several strategies
with different assets, rates and withdrawal terms.

## Position

A position is a wallet's holding in a strategy. Its source normally identifies
the signing wallet by `ownerAddress`, without registering it in SDP. An existing
connected wallet can instead use its `walletId`. Both use the same API.

Imported holdings have unknown acquisition history and cost basis. A current
balance alone does not establish earned yield.

## Operation

An operation tracks one deposit or withdrawal through preparation, wallet
signing, chain submission and economic delivery. Before submit acceptance,
replacing unsigned terms requires a new action identity and review. After
acceptance, refresh and cancellation are unavailable; track the outcome.

A signature, confirmed request or escrowed share balance is not a payout.
`completed` requires verified delivery. Unknown outcomes retain their original
signed bytes and remain unresolved until evidence establishes the outcome.
