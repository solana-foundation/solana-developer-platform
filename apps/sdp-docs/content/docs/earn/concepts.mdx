## Strategy

A strategy is an entry in the Earn catalogue. Its provider may implement a
vault, lending market or RWA product. A protocol can expose several strategies
with different assets, rates and withdrawal terms.

## Position

A position is a wallet's holding in a strategy. Its source identifies a
registered `walletId` or an `ownerAddress`. That identity preserves wallet
visibility and ownership checks while both sources use the same API.

Imported holdings have unknown acquisition history and cost basis. A current
balance alone does not establish earned yield.

## Operation

An operation tracks one deposit or withdrawal across reviewed actions, signing,
provider approval, chain submission and economic delivery. A later transaction
requires a new action identity and review under the same operation.

An approval, confirmed request or escrowed share balance is not a payout.
`completed` requires verified delivery. Unknown outcomes retain their original
signed bytes and remain unresolved until evidence establishes the outcome.
