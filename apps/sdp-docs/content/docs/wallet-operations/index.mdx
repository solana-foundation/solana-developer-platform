## Provider policy checks

For supported BYOK wallets, the connection and wallet detail pages show
an advisory policy state. SDP checks after connection and wallet creation, then
hourly. A missing policy warns without blocking signing. A failed or denied read
shows **Provider policy not verified**. Review the policy in the provider before
sending funds.

`GET /v1/wallets/{walletId}` includes `providerPolicy` for these wallets, including
when `includeBalance=false`. It contains `status` (`present`, `missing`, or
`unknown`), `reason`, and nullable `checkedAt`. Managed wallets omit this field.
`present` means the provider reports a policy attached to the wallet or a native
transfer rule covering its vault and supported assets. It does not
verify policy strength or permission to sign. Replaced credentials and results
older than two hours are `unknown`.
