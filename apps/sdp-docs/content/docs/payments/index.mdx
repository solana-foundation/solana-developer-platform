---
title: Payments
description: Send, accept and reconcile Solana payments from your SDP custody wallets.
---

Send SOL and SPL tokens from your custody wallets, pay many recipients in one batch, schedule recurring payments, and match what moved to your own records. If you have no custody wallet yet, start with [Set Up Wallets](/docs/guides/setup-wallets).

## What's in this section

- **[How payments work on SDP](/docs/payments/concepts)**: wallets, tokens, fees and the transfer record.
- **Send payments**
  - [Basic payment](/docs/payments/send-basic-payment): one transfer, from the dashboard or the API.
  - [Payment with memo](/docs/payments/send-payment-with-memo): tag a transfer with your order or invoice reference.
  - [Payouts and disbursements](/docs/payments/send-payouts): pay many recipients and reconcile the run.
- **Accept payments**
  - [Accept overview](/docs/payments/accept-overview): payment links, shared addresses, and finding each payment.
  - [Verifying a payment](/docs/payments/accept-verification): transfer statuses and when a payment is final.
  - [Indexing and reconciliation](/docs/payments/accept-indexing): list transfers and match them to your ledger.

Wallet balances and controls are in [Wallet operations](/docs/wallet-operations).

## Underlying API

Payments endpoints live under `/v1/payments`: transfers, transfer batches, recurring payments and wallet balances. Each page here links the endpoints it uses, and the [Payments API reference](/docs/reference/api/payments) lists them all.
