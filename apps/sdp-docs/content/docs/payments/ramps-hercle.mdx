Hercle is one of SDP's ramp providers. Its capabilities sit beside the others' in the [capability matrix](/docs/payments/ramps-providers#capability-matrix); quotes go through [Ramps](/docs/payments/ramps) with `"provider": "hercle"`.

A Sandbox project uses the `HERCLE_SANDBOX_*` keys and a Production project the `HERCLE_PRODUCTION_*` keys. Hercle runs in a mode only while all three of its keys are set and fit that mode; otherwise it answers `PROVIDER_NOT_CONFIGURED`. Hercle is offered in sandbox only for now: its bank settlement is still simulated, so a production project does not see it.

## Configuration

| Variable | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `HERCLE_SANDBOX_CLIENT_ID` | conditional | Sandbox client id (`hpk_test_…`). Every request carries it with an HMAC-SHA256 signature over the timestamp, method, path and body. A live client id is refused before any request. |
| `HERCLE_SANDBOX_SECRET_KEY` | conditional | Sandbox signing secret for that client id. |
| `HERCLE_SANDBOX_WEBHOOK_PUBLIC_KEY` | conditional | Hercle's ECDSA P-256 public key (SPKI PEM) for the endpoint Hercle calls at `/webhooks/payments/ramps/sandbox/hercle`. It verifies `X-Signature` over `X-Timestamp.rawBody`. |
| `HERCLE_PRODUCTION_CLIENT_ID` | conditional | Production client id (`hpk_live_…`). A sandbox client id is refused before any request. |
| `HERCLE_PRODUCTION_SECRET_KEY` | conditional | Production signing secret. |
| `HERCLE_PRODUCTION_WEBHOOK_PUBLIC_KEY` | conditional | Public key for the endpoint Hercle calls at `/webhooks/payments/ramps/production/hercle`. |

## Business onboarding

Hercle serves business counterparties registered in Switzerland or the EEA. Each business becomes its own Hercle account, which SDP opens and verifies on the business's behalf; every order then acts for that account. The counterparty is Hercle's customer, and the project's custody wallet is only the crypto side of the ramp.

1. `GET /v1/counterparties/{counterpartyId}/requirements` answers `collect` with the registered company name, company registration number, country of registration, registered address, and the business's acceptance of Hercle's Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy (`consent` fields, accepted with the literal `true`).
2. Submitting those fields opens the Hercle account and starts its verification. The answer is `customer_verification_required` with a hosted `verificationUrl`, minted for that answer and never stored.
3. While Hercle reviews the business the answer is `customer_verifying`, and `customer_verification_failed` if Hercle declines it. Read the requirements again to follow it: each read asks Hercle for its current verdict and never starts anything. If a submission opened the account but stopped before its verification started, the read answers `collect` again, and the next submission starts it.
4. Once Hercle approves the business the answer is `ready`, and quotes run. A quote for a business Hercle has not approved is refused with `409 CONFLICT` before Hercle is called.

The registered company name is the name Hercle's bank expects on the wire, so give the legal name, not the counterparty's display name.

SDP stores the Hercle account id and its verification status, never the collected details.

## Quotes and settlement

Hercle is on-ramp only: EUR, USD and GBP into `usdc.solana`, `usdt.solana` and `sol.solana`. A quote is a Hercle order with `deliveryMode: "manual_instructions"`. Its `fiat_funding` instruction names the business's own account at Hercle's bank, the payment reference the wire must carry, and `payerAccountHolder`, the business itself. The bank returns a wire from any other account, and the order fails. `expiresAt` is when the order's funding window closes.

Hercle's refusals of the business answer `409 CONFLICT`: not verified, suspended, or its bank account still opening. A currency pair Hercle does not offer answers `400 BAD_REQUEST`.

Hercle reports the order's progress by webhook, and SDP also asks Hercle for the order when a webhook is missed. A settled order names the transaction that delivered the crypto and moves the transfer to `settling`. It completes once SDP finds that delivery on chain.

In a Sandbox project, `POST /v1/payments/ramps/sandbox/simulate` has Hercle settle the order as its bank would, from the moment it is quoted. The transfer then moves through the same webhook and status pull as a real wire. A Sandbox project runs on devnet, which has no USDT, so `usdt.solana` is refused there with `400` before Hercle is called.
