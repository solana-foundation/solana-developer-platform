Mainnet access requires approval for your organization. Signing up gives you a
Sandbox project on devnet; the SDP team enables Production on mainnet after
reviewing your request.

## 1. Request mainnet access

Sign in to the [dashboard](https://platform.solana.com/dashboard) as an
**organization admin**. Open your account menu at the bottom of the sidebar,
choose **Settings**, and find **Mainnet access**. Click **Request mainnet access**.
If the section is missing, ask an organization admin to submit the request.

[![The Mainnet access section in organization settings, with Request mainnet access](/images/go-to-mainnet/01-request-access.png)](/docs/images/go-to-mainnet/01-request-access.png)

Select your organization's registered **Country or region**, then click
**Continue**. Check this carefully: changes after submission go through the SDP
team.

[![Request mainnet access with Singapore selected as the organization's jurisdiction](/images/go-to-mainnet/02-jurisdiction.png)](/docs/images/go-to-mainnet/02-jurisdiction.png)

Read the **Terms and Conditions** to the end, accept them on your organization's
behalf, then click **Send request**. **Request sent** confirms submission. The
settings card shows **In review** until the SDP team approves it.

[![Request sent confirmation, with SDP review still pending](/images/go-to-mainnet/request-sent.png)](/docs/images/go-to-mainnet/request-sent.png)

If you see **Request not sent**, use **Send request again**. Your details are
already saved. If requests aren't open yet, contact the SDP team.

## 2. Switch to Production

After approval, refresh **Settings**. Once **Mainnet access** shows **Approved**,
open the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar and select your production
project, named **Default Production Project** by default.

Production runs on `mainnet-beta`. Its wallets, provider connections, and API keys
are separate from Sandbox; your sandbox resources are not copied across.

## 3. Connect your Privy account

Production requires **Bring your own credentials**. With Production selected,
open **Wallets**, click **Create wallet**, select **Privy**, and click **Continue**.

Enter a **Credential label**, **Privy app ID**, and **Privy app secret** from your
[Privy dashboard](https://dashboard.privy.io). Add an **Initial wallet label**,
then click **Connect and verify**. SDP verifies the connection and creates the
first wallet in your Privy account.

[![Privy credential form with an example app ID and a masked app secret](/images/go-to-mainnet/04-connect-privy.png)](/docs/images/go-to-mainnet/04-connect-privy.png)

For more wallets, choose your connection when creating a wallet. See
[Create a wallet](/docs/guides/setup-wallets).

## 4. Create a production API key

Still in Production, open **API keys** and click **New API key**. Name the key,
grant the actions your integration needs, select the wallets it may sign with,
and review the summary before clicking **Create key**.

[![API key review showing Production, selected permissions, and the Treasury wallet](/images/go-to-mainnet/05-production-api-key.png)](/docs/images/go-to-mainnet/05-production-api-key.png)

Copy the key when it appears and save it in your secret manager. It is shown
once. Replace your sandbox key in your backend; the API URL stays
`https://api.solana.com`. The key's project determines the network. See
[API key grants and wallet access](/docs/developing-with-sdp/manage-api-keys).

## 5. Fund your wallet with mainnet USDC

Send a small amount of **USDC on Solana mainnet** to your new wallet's address.
Devnet tokens have no value on mainnet. Circle's
[mainnet USDC mint](https://developers.circle.com/stablecoins/usdc-contract-addresses)
is `EPjFWdd5AufqSSqeM2qN1xzybapC8G4wEGGkZwyTDt1v`.

Use `"token": "USDC"` in transfer requests so SDP resolves the mint for your
project's network. Replace any hardcoded devnet mint addresses.

SDP sponsors network fees and recipient token-account rent for the transfers it
signs from your Privy wallet. Fund it with the USDC you want to send; no SOL is
needed for those fees. Sponsorship limits still apply. If sponsorship is
unavailable, SDP refuses the transfer rather than charging your wallet.

Send a [small first payment](/docs/payments/send-basic-payment) and
[verify its status](/docs/payments/accept-verification) before increasing volume.
