Embedded Yield puts Earn inside your app: customers browse strategies, deposit
from their own wallets and watch their balances, while your backend calls SDP.
[Run the example](https://github.com/solana-foundation/solana-developer-platform/tree/main/examples/earn-quickstart)
to see the flow, then follow the [quickstart](/docs/earn/quickstart).

## Who does what

- **Your backend** holds the SDP API key and makes every Earn call. Never send
  the key to the browser.
- **Your app** connects the customer's wallet (Privy, Wallet Standard or any
  other), shows the terms and asks the wallet to sign.
- **SDP** prepares each transaction, checks the signed bytes, broadcasts them
  and tracks settlement.

## The flow

1. List strategies with `GET /v1/earn/discovery` and show the ones accepting
   deposits.
2. Prepare the deposit with the customer's wallet as `source.ownerAddress`.
3. Show `terms`, have the wallet sign `nextAction.transaction`, and submit it.
4. Poll the operation until it is `completed`, then show the customer's
   `GET /v1/earn/positions?ownerAddress=...`.

Withdrawals follow the same steps with a `positionId`. Before you handle
retries, refresh or cancellation, read [integration details](/docs/earn/integration-details);
to scope reads per customer, see
[one integration, many customers](/docs/earn/concepts#one-integration-many-customers).
