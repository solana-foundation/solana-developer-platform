Everything you do in SDP belongs to an **organization**, your team's workspace. An organization holds **projects**, and each project has its own API keys, wallets, and members. This guide creates your account and organization.

SDP runs in two places, each with its own accounts, organizations, and API keys:

| | Dashboard | API | Network |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Devnet sandbox | [platform-beta.solana.com](https://platform-beta.solana.com) | `https://api-beta.solana.com` | devnet, open to everyone |
| Mainnet | [platform.solana.com](https://platform.solana.com/dashboard) | `https://api.solana.com` | mainnet, after SDP approves your organization |

Start in the Devnet sandbox. Nothing there holds real value. Request mainnet when your integration works there; see [Go to mainnet](/docs/guides/go-to-mainnet).

### 1. Create your account

Go to [platform-beta.solana.com](https://platform-beta.solana.com) and click **Create account**. The sign-in screen has the same button at the top right. On platform.solana.com, **Try the sandbox** leads to the same place.

Enter your email address and a password to create your account.

### 2. Create your organization

After you sign in, **Name your organization** asks for an **Organization name**. Enter it and click **Create organization**. You become the organization's admin. If you've been invited to an organization, accept the invitation or pick the organization from the list instead.

To switch organizations or projects later, click your organization's name at the top of the sidebar.

### 3. Work in a project

Your organization comes with **Default Sandbox Project** on devnet. Build there. The switcher's **Production** row opens platform.solana.com, where mainnet lives. To read projects with an API key, see the [Projects API reference](/docs/reference/api/projects).
