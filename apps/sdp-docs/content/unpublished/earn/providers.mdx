Choose a strategy from your project's
[catalogue](/docs/reference/api/earn/list-earn-strategies). Check funding,
eligibility, and exit terms before depositing.
Use `sourceKind` to distinguish
[DeFi and real-world assets](/docs/earn/concepts#defi-and-real-world-assets).

## Check availability

| Check | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| `status: "active"` | The strategy has not paused or stopped new deposits. |
| `fundable: true` | The instrument exists on your project's cluster. |
| `depositMints[0]` | The direct deposit's native token mint. |
| `depositSlippage` | Whether the build requires a quote-derived share floor. |
| Preview with `blockingIssues: []` | No reported provider condition blocks the quoted operation. |

Execution also requires deployment support, organization access, and any
required provider approval. A
[deposit preview](/docs/reference/api/earn/create-earn-vault-deposit-preview)
neither reserves liquidity nor guarantees a build.

Sandbox uses devnet; production uses mainnet-beta. The catalogue defaults to
your project's cluster. In sandbox, `?cluster=mainnet-beta` returns a mirrored
mainnet shelf with `fundable: false`, without enabling deposits.

## Confirm eligibility

SDP access authorizes API calls, not investment. For restricted products, confirm
investor, jurisdiction, wallet, subscription, and redemption requirements with
the provider.

Check eligibility for each end investor and the wallet receiving the position.

An SDP API key cannot replace that approval. One usable strategy does not make
the provider permissionless.

## Follow the funds

| Strategy shape | Money in | Money out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vault or lending position | A transaction exchanges the deposit token for shares or receipt tokens. | Shares redeem under the provider's liquidity and exit rules. |
| Yield-bearing token | A transaction acquires the token in the owner's wallet. | Sales depend on market liquidity; redemption may need separate provider approval. |
| Provider order | A transaction records a payment or redemption leg. | The provider must complete economic settlement later. |

Read each position's [withdrawal options](/docs/earn/withdrawals#1-choose-an-exit).
Market sales and issuer redemptions differ in eligibility, pricing, timing,
and liquidity. An RWA classification alone does not tell you which exit applies.

## Show rates and risk

`currentApy: "0.062"` means 6.2%. If it is absent, show an unavailable rate,
not zero. APY describes a rate, not a guaranteed return.
`redemptionDelayDays` is expected timing, not a guaranteed deadline.

Use current risk metadata and provider terms. Holding tokens in your own wallet
does not guarantee principal protection, segregated backing, or liquidity.

Paused deposits or delisting do not hide existing positions. Use their
[position IDs to withdraw](/docs/earn/withdrawals), subject to provider capability
and liquidity.

Make your first deposit with the [sandbox quickstart](/docs/earn/quickstart).
