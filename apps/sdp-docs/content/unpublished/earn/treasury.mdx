title: Treasury
description: Discover strategies and manage your organization's holdings through the shared Earn operation flow.
---

Treasury uses the [Core Earn API](./core-api.mdx). It has no separate yield
endpoints or program-allocation model.

Use a registered `walletId` as the funding source. The wallet keeps its custody
provider, signing permissions, approval policy and fee configuration. Preparation
returns terms for review; execution queues work for the same asynchronous
worker used by every Earn client.

Discovery, position reads, operation history and withdrawals use the shared
contract. Approval tracking survives the request and browser session. Queued
exits remain under one operation until delivery or a verified cancellation.
