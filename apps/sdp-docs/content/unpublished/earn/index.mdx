Put treasury funds to work or offer yield inside your app. Earn connects
strategy discovery, deposits, withdrawals, and reporting through one API.

</ActionLinks>

## What you can build

| Feature | What Earn provides |
| --- | --- |
| [Strategy discovery](/docs/earn/providers) | Providers, deposit assets, rates, eligibility, and exit options. |
| [Deposits and withdrawals](/docs/earn/deposit-api) | Previews, transaction builds, submission, and settlement tracking. |
| [Balances and earnings](/docs/earn/reporting) | Live positions, recorded movements, and available return calculations. |
| [Wallet choice](/docs/earn/concepts#who-signs) | Customer wallet signing or your organization's configured custody provider. |

Returns, losses, fees, and withdrawal availability depend on the strategy.

## Flow of funds

<EarnFlow>

- **Owner's wallet**

  Sends the strategy's supported stablecoin.

- **Protocol or asset**

  Funds buy shares or yield-bearing tokens for the owner's wallet.

- **Owner's wallet**

  Receives the payout when the withdrawal settles.

</EarnFlow>

<Callout type="info">
Funds move through the strategy's protocol, trading route, or provider. SDP
does not pool them in an SDP-owned account. Provider custody and withdrawal
escrow risks still apply.
</Callout>

[How Earn works](/docs/earn/concepts) explains shares, signing, and settlement.
