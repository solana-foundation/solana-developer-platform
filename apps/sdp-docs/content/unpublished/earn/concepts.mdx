A strategy puts stablecoins into a vault, lending market, or yield-bearing
token. Your wallet holds the resulting position until you withdraw.

## DeFi and real-world assets

| Source | Where yield comes from |
| --- | --- |
| DeFi (`defi`) | Onchain lending or vault strategies, such as Kamino and Jupiter Lend. Returns depend on protocol activity. |
| Real-world assets (`rwa`) | Tokenized investments outside crypto. For example, Ondo USDY is a note linked to US Treasuries and bank deposits; issuer terms apply. |

The catalogue's `sourceKind` describes the yield source, not how a deposit or
withdrawal settles. An RWA token can trade in one transaction; another product
may need a provider order. Check each position's
[withdrawal options](/docs/earn/withdrawals#1-choose-an-exit).

## Three amounts to understand

| Amount | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Shares (`shares`) | How much of the position you own, in share units. |
| Share value (`tokenValue`) | The shares' current value in the deposit token. |
| Withdrawable shares (`withdrawableShares`) | The live ceiling for an exit, given locks and liquidity. |

Shares and stablecoins are different units. A withdrawal takes a share amount;
its stablecoin payout depends on the provider's live state. Use the position's
live values and a [withdrawal preview](/docs/earn/withdrawals#2-preview-and-submit)
rather than calculating a payout from a requested deposit or an old exchange
rate. Missing live values mean unavailable, not zero.

## Where funds go

A vault deposit sends stablecoins through a transaction and returns shares or
receipt tokens to the owner's wallet. A token strategy instead acquires a
yield-bearing token; its exit may sell that token or use a provider redemption.
Never send tokens directly to a strategy's `providerReference`: it identifies
the instrument, not a funding address. Use a
[deposit transaction](/docs/earn/quickstart).

The deposited stablecoins go to the protocol or provider, not an SDP-owned
pooled account. Holding shares or tokens in your wallet does not remove the
provider's custody or withdrawal-escrow risks.

The position's value can rise or fall with returns, losses, and fees. An
observed APY is not a guaranteed return, and owning a token does not guarantee
principal protection or immediate liquidity.

## Who signs

SDP builds the transaction, the customer's wallet signs it, and your backend
submits it to SDP. The customer's wallet holds the position and receives the
withdrawal payout. See [Embedded Yield](/docs/guides/embedded-yield) for the
backend integration.

Keep the API key on your backend. Embedded Yield never sends the customer's
private key to SDP.

Requests use the project's environment: sandbox runs on devnet, production on
mainnet-beta. Strategy availability and investor eligibility still apply.

## When a withdrawal is complete

An instant exit can transfer tokens in one transaction. Other routes create a
provider order or a separate withdrawal request and pay later. A confirmed
request does not prove that funds arrived. Track
[settlement and the observed payout](/docs/earn/reporting#track-movements-through-settlement)
before crediting a balance or sending a payment.
