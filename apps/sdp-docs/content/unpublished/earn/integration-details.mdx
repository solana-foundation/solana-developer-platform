Use these rules with the [Embedded Yield flow](/docs/guides/embedded-yield).

## Quote protection

Read the strategy's policies and resolve the preview's `blockingIssues`
before building.

| Policy | When non-null | When null |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `depositSlippage` | Derive and send `minSharesOut` from live `sharesOut`. | Omit the share floor. |
| `withdrawalSlippage` | Derive and send `minAmountOut` from live `assetsOut`. | Omit the token floor. |

A null policy can mean the instruction cannot enforce a floor, including
later-priced provider orders. Do not invent one or promise unsupported price
protection. Previews estimate an outcome; they do not reserve an exchange rate.

Calculate the floor with integer base units:

1. Parse a positive quote at `shareDecimals` for deposits or `assetDecimals`
   for withdrawals. Reject significant precision beyond that scale.
2. Multiply by `(10_000 - toleranceBps)`, divide by `10_000`, and round down.
   Keep at least one base unit.
3. Convert back to a decimal string. Never round-trip amounts through a
   JavaScript `number`.

The [backend module](/docs/guides/embedded-yield#get-the-backend-module)
includes `floorForTolerance` when the selected strategy requires a floor.

## Fees and sponsorship

Customers pay network fees and account rent in SOL by default, including on
a first stablecoin deposit that creates an account. Rent is separate from
principal and yield.

To sponsor a customer's transaction:

1. Set your backend-controlled `feePayer` when building.
2. Have the customer sign first.
3. Verify the unchanged message and expected payer, then co-sign the same
   transaction. Submit both signatures.

Changing the payer requires a new build and signatures. The
[backend module](/docs/guides/embedded-yield#get-the-backend-module) provides
`signEarnTransaction` to check message equality and `createSponsorSigner` to
verify the payer.

- Only sign SDP builds initiated for an authenticated customer.
- Bound the signer's SOL float, rate-limit sponsorship, and monitor spend.
- Store signing credentials in your secret manager.

The catalogue's `feeSponsored` does not apply to customer-wallet transactions.
Use your own `feePayer` to sponsor them.

Full Kamino exits leave the share account open. Rent recovery requires a
separate owner-authorized close. Do not promise customers or sponsors an
automatic refund.

## Swap-funded deposits

Set `sourceTokenMint` to fund a different deposit token through Jupiter using
a supported source stablecoin. Routes require mainnet and cannot be tested
in sandbox. Normally, swap and deposit share one transaction.

If `requiresSeparateSwap: true`, the response supplies `swap` and `followUp`:

1. Save the swap's signed bytes, signature, and follow-up body durably.
2. Broadcast the standalone swap yourself and confirm it.
3. Build the follow-up deposit with its original floor and fee payer.

SDP does not record the standalone swap as an Earn movement. Your app owns
its recovery. Reconcile an unresolved swap before starting another.

## Retry safely

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| An unsigned build expires. | Build again before requesting a signature. |
| Submit times out or returns an uncertain server error. | Retry the exact signed body and original key. |
| A signed direct deposit or withdrawal reaches SDP after expiry. | Submit the original intent and track it; expiry cannot prove it never landed. |
| A key or build was consumed with different terms. | Find the original action before creating a replacement. |
| Preview has blocking issues. | Resolve them and preview again. |

- Submit queued requests and cancellations promptly through SDP.
- If a wallet broadcasts one itself, submitting after the build expires may
  fail to restore tracking.
- Keep unresolved operations visible and escalate a missing request. Do not
  assume no funds moved.
